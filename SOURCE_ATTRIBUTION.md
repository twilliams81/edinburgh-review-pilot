# Source attribution and limitations

Four provisional article transcriptions from the Edinburgh Review, derived from Internet Archive source issues. Each article's metadata records its source identifier and URL, source date, original catalogue date, printed opening page and registered text hash.

The display titles come from the research catalogue. Earlier agent-assisted source reviews checked article extraction. Current preparation checked file identity and catalogue status; it did not perform fresh full-text proofreading. OCR mistakes, page furniture and layout losses may remain. Source scans are linked, not copied into this package. No licence is asserted here for third-party scan images.

The four articles are illustrative selections, not a probability sample, a complete issue set or an evaluated fiction-classification result. The full corpus's classification is pending. The 1832 source issue is dated April in the earlier source review although its Internet Archive identifier contains January; both dates are retained in metadata.

Reading view changes display whitespace only. Downloaded texts retain all registered bytes, including footnotes. Consult source scans for quotation and source-critical work.
